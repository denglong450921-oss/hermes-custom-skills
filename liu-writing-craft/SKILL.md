## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

- **写作是认知肌肉训练**: Use writing when you need to clarify thought, not merely produce text. AI can handle routine drafting, but judgment, taste, question design, and revision require hand practice.
- **作文到论文的成人礼**: Treat the move from school composition to academic writing as a change of game: from standard answers to original questions, from closed-book display to open-book inquiry, from expression to proof.
- **说理写作五能力**: Build observation and questioning, data search and digestion, analysis and argumentation, integration, and reader-centered communication. If one is weak, the essay will show the weakness as style, structure, or vagueness.
- **透明玻璃文风**: Prefer prose that lets readers see the idea, not the author's ornament. Clear prose uses short enough sentences, concrete subjects, clean paragraphing, and visible logic.
- **三种坏腔调**: Watch for student-style ornament, academic obscurity, and bureaucratic evasion. All three protect the writer from responsibility and shift the cost to the reader.
- **问题是文章的引擎**: Start with a question before collecting material. A good question creates direction; an unclear question produces piles of notes and no argument.
- **三步提问法**: Move from field to topic to question. Narrow the scope until the question can be answered with available evidence and expressed clearly to a non-specialist.
- **谜题测试**: A puzzle is a gap, contradiction, or surprise that makes readers want an explanation. Express it with "同样是 X，为什么有的 A，有的 B？"
- **变量思维**: Compare differences and search for mechanisms. Identify the dependent variable, possible independent variables, controls, and plausible mechanisms before writing.
- **选题四象限**: Judge questions by size and significance. Beginners should often start with small manageable questions, then connect them to larger meaning.
- **死胡同测试**: Before committing, ask whether the question is clear, answerable, feasible, time-bounded, and ethically sound.
- **意义四把尺子**: Evaluate relevance, time scale, research contribution, and distance from human well-being. Meaning is not just grandeur; it is consequential connection.
- **旗杆式知识结构**: Read broadly enough to communicate across domains, but anchor knowledge around a few deep poles. Breadth without poles becomes trivia; depth without breadth becomes tunnel vision.
- **文献是对话场**: Literature review is not a reading list. It answers "they say what?" and prepares "I say what?" by mapping positions, gaps, methods, and evidence.
- **文献五问**: For each source ask: motivation, question, argument, method/evidence, and relation to my question. This turns reading into usable notes.
- **论证树形结构**: A paper grows from question/root to claim/trunk, reasons/branches, and evidence/leaves. Weak evidence or missing reasons makes the tree collapse.
- **结构降低写作难度**: Structure is a scaffold, not a cage. Use macro-structure for the whole paper, meso-structure for sections and paragraphs, and micro-structure for words and sentences.
- **标题是高速路广告牌**: The title must be specific, understandable, and sticky enough to stop a scanning reader.
- **十遍法则**: Spend disproportionate revision effort on abstract and introduction. They create the reader's first judgment and often decide whether the paper gets read.
- **懒人读者设定**: Assume readers are distracted, tired, and impatient. Give them topic sentences, short paragraphs, headings, transitions, and visible stakes.
- **学术故事 LOCK**: Good academic stories have a lead character/topic, objective, confrontation, and knockout ending. They create curiosity while staying evidence-based.
- **框架效应**: How material is framed changes meaning. Choose angle, scale, sequence, and emphasis deliberately; framing is not decoration but argument.
- **读者放在肩膀上**: Shift from author-centered writing to reader-centered design. Readers lack your background, intention, and patience; bridge that gap.
- **先有后好**: Overcome writing blockage by producing rough material first, then revising. Use speaking, freewriting, and attention management to restart motion.
- **反馈回路**: Improvement requires writing, feedback, and adjustment. Ask specific questions, make it easy for reviewers to respond, and keep a writing archive.
