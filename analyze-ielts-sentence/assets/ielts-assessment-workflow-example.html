从理解到迁移：货物运输增长句

先拆清句子的范围、主干与因果关系，再通过八道题检查自己能否真正提取并迁移这个表达。

As far as goods transport is concerned, growth is due to a large extent to changes in the European economy and its system of production.

Study

1. 学习材料

学习顺序是“理解 → 拆解 → 升级 → 迁移”。复杂度逐步增加，但核心逻辑始终只有一个：某一领域的增长主要源于系统变化。

核心意思：就货物运输而言，其增长在很大程度上是由欧洲经济及其生产体系的变化造成的。写作功能是限定讨论范围，并解释增长原因。

从主干到完整句

主干

Growth is due to changes.

结果 + 系动结构 + 原因。

范围

As far as goods transport is concerned, ...

把讨论限定在货物运输，不是在谈所有经济增长。

程度

... is due to a large extent to ...

表示“主要由于”，但没有绝对排除其他原因。

原因

changes in the European economy and its system of production

its 指向 the European economy，把经济与生产体系联系起来。

高频表达

as far as X is concerned
就 X 而言；用于限定论述范围。
be due largely to
主要由于；比冗长的 be due to a large extent to 更紧凑。
be attributed largely to
主要归因于；适合正式分析原因，但不要把所有原因都归结为一个因素。
structural changes
结构性变化；强调深层、系统性的变化。

表达升级

Clear

Freight transport has grown because the European economy and its production system have changed.

IELTS-ready

The expansion of freight transport can be attributed largely to structural changes in Europe’s economy and production systems.

段落中的用法

The expansion of freight transport can be attributed largely to structural changes in Europe’s economy and production systems. Modern manufacturers often divide production across several specialised locations, so components must travel repeatedly before a final product reaches consumers. For example, a vehicle may contain parts produced in several different countries. As a result, economic integration increases not only trade in finished goods but also the movement of intermediate products.
